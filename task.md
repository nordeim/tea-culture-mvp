Key design decisions:

**🎨 Color Palette Applied**
- Tea green (`#5C8A4D`) for nature, freshness, and primary accents
- Warm ivory (`#FAF6EE`, `#F5F0E8`) for paper-textured backgrounds
- Terracotta (`#C4724B`) for warmth and fermentation-level coding
- Dark brown (`#3D2B1F`) for authority and heritage text
- Gold (`#C5A55A`) as the signature accent for premium feel — used in lines, badges, and CTAs

**🏛️ Page Structure**
1. **Hero** — Full-screen tea garden photo with layered gradient overlay, animated steam wisps, and scroll indicator
2. **Philosophy** — Split layout with ceremony image (steam animation) + 4 value icons
3. **Collection** — 3-tab interface (By Origin / Fermentation / Season) with product cards
4. **Tea Culture** — Dark section with immersive overlay cards + temperature guide strip
5. **Macro Feature** — Leaf texture close-up with terroir storytelling
6. **Subscription** — 3-tier pricing with highlighted popular option
7. **Testimonials** — Community quotes with gold star ratings
8. **CTA** — Green tea-colored call to action with trust badges
9. **Newsletter** — Functional email form with inline confirmation
10. **Footer** — 4-column layout with social links

**✨ Functional Interactions**
- Tab switching for product organization
- Mobile hamburger menu with smooth toggle
- Navbar transparency → frosted glass on scroll
- Scroll-reveal animations via IntersectionObserver
- Toast notifications for subscriptions and newsletter
- Back-to-top button appears on scroll
